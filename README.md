## Five terms that change what you actually pay

**Renewal price is often not on the sales page.** HostPapa advertises "from $4.45/mo" for the first term and does not publish what follows. If a provider will not show you the renewal rate without a cart, assume it is at least double and go build the cart.

**Longer terms are not automatically cheaper.** Bluehost and HostGator apply their extra 15% to 25% only on 12-month terms, while their everyday 24-month rates are lower per month. Run both numbers. On HostGator, the 24-month Snappy price of $2.09 beats a 12-month term with 15% off in most configurations, because the discount is applied to a higher base.

**Discount codes can kill your refund.** EDIS Global's Black Friday offer takes 25% off annual VPS orders at eight locations on top of an 11+1 month deal, which works out to 31.25% less than monthly billing, and it throws in a one-time 1 TB traffic pool credit instead of the usual 100 GB. Orders that use the discount code are explicitly non-refundable. The same page limits you to one location change within 24 hours, and Vienna is restricted to NDC2.

**Stock limits are real.** VM6 sells a fixed number of slots at its Black Friday prices and states plainly that sold-out plans will not be restocked. The 14-day money-back guarantee still applies, but only if you get an order in.

**The control panel is a line item.** ScalaHosting includes its own SPanel at no extra cost. HostGator's VPS plans are self-managed and do not include cPanel. On hosts that do offer cPanel, it can add $15 or more per month, which can exceed the discount you came for.

## FAQ

**Is Black Friday actually the cheapest time to buy a VPS?**
Not reliably. HostArmada's current birthday sale starts at $3.28/mo for yearly VPS, below the $3.69 it offered during its 2025 Black Friday event. Hostinger's KVM 1 sits at $6.49 against roughly $4.99 reported last year. A sale that has been live since October 10 is a price, not a discount.

**Should I wait for November 27?**
Only if a specific provider you want is still on a waitlist. Contabo, netcup, Kinsta, 20i and several others have sale pages up without prices. Kinsta's countdown pointed at 08:00 UTC on November 9, so that page is worth a fresh look today. Everything else already live has an end date, and several end before the Black Friday weekend.

**Is a 24-month prepay worth it?**
Run the four-year total. On Hostinger's KVM 1 that is $443.52, with the monthly rate climbing 84.7% at renewal. On a provider that locks the promotional rate, a long term is a genuine saving. On one that resets to list price, you are trading flexibility for a discount that expires.

**Can I stack a Black Friday code with other offers?**
Usually not. VM6's codes are one per customer, valid on new orders only, and cannot be combined with other codes. EDIS applies its 25% on top of the 11+1 month structure but makes those orders non-refundable. Read the conditions block, not the banner.

**Do I need cPanel?**
Only if you specifically want it. ScalaHosting includes SPanel free; HostGator's VPS plans exclude cPanel entirely. Adding a commercial panel licence can cost $15 or more per month, which changes the comparison more than most discounts do.
